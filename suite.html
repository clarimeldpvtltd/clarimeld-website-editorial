Why it matters

Keep the reason with the work.

In an established software company, the decision, the explanation and the customer update are made by different teams, often in different tools. Suite lets the reason travel with the work, wherever you choose to link it.

  • Product manager · PM OSMakes and records the decision, and why.
  • Technical writer · Writer OSPrepares and reviews the explanation from the agreed context.
  • Customer-facing team · Public OSChooses what to communicate, and when.

Nobody has to use all three, and each link is optional. Already use ChatGPT or Claude? Keep using them. Suite carries the decision and its reason between teams, and a person acts at every handoff.

Follow one example

Northline Workflows is a fictional, established B2B software provider with enterprise customers in several regions. Those customers want more flexible approval controls, and different regions have different needs. Northline's team has to decide what to build first, explain it accurately and tell customers what to expect. Regional approval paths are Northline's feature, not a Clarimeld feature. This is an illustrative example, not a customer story: every name, quote and status is made up. The steps run in time order and do not show how any step is built.

Before the decision

1. Customers ask

Customers describe what they need in their own words. A vote is a signal, not a promise.

Starts in Public OS, or from your own notes

Optional branch: Discussion and moderation

Your team merges duplicates and keeps threads on topic.

Before the decision

2. Frame the problem

A product manager reads the evidence side by side. Some needs pull in different directions, so the first job is to understand the problem, not to create an idea.

Optional link: Public OS → PM OS

PM OS works from your own notes and tickets too. It does not need Public OS.

The decision

3. Choose, and record why

The team compares two approaches and records the choice, the reason and the option it set aside. Deferring is a recorded reason, not a promise to build it later.

Inside PM OS

The decision

4. Context a colleague can pick up

The record keeps the decision, the scope, the open questions and the deferred option together. An engineer or designer can read it without finding the right chat thread.

Inside PM OS

Optional branch: Delivery coordination

Teams can track dependencies and release milestones so people coordinate. Clarimeld does not build or deploy anything.

Before sharing

5. Start from the agreed context

A technical writer starts from a brief and keeps the decision summary beside the draft, instead of asking what was decided.

Optional link: PM OS → Writer OS

Writer OS projects start from a brief. They do not need a PM OS record.

Before sharing

6. Draft, then a person reviews

The writer drafts with the decision in view, then a person reviews. Here the reviewer spots a sentence that implies custom rule scripting is available, checks it against the deferred scope, and corrects it before anything is published.

Inside Writer OS

Optional branch: Advisory flags

Writer OS describes advisory voice and scope flags for editors. They are advice. People verify claims and citations.

Customer update

7. Choose what to share

The customer-facing team decides what customers should see. Here: a clear status, a drafted update, and one item held back for now.

Optional link: Writer OS → Public OS

Optional branch: Curated roadmap

Leaders choose which items customers can see. A roadmap card shows intention, not a delivery date.

Later, when the release is ready

8. A reviewed update is published

When the release is ready, a person reviews the update and publishes it. Publishing does not email anyone by itself.

Inside Public OS

You can also write an update directly in Public OS.

Optional branch: Who hears

Optionally, a person reviews the eligible recipients and then sends. Requesters, followers and other eligible recipients are separate groups.

After the update

9. Feedback comes back

A customer replies. A person decides whether it belongs in discovery. It is feedback for the next round, not proof the release worked, and not a commitment.

Optional link: Public OS → PM OS

Optional branch: Not every reply starts new work

A person decides which replies become evidence for a future cycle.
