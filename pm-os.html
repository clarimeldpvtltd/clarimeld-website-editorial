Why it matters

Make the call, and be able to explain it.

In an established software company, feedback arrives through sales calls, support tickets and chat threads, and different customers want different things. PM OS helps you see those needs together, decide, and give everyone the reasoning.

  • For youWeigh competing needs side by side, make the call, and say why.
  • For your teamEngineering and design read the decision, scope and open questions in one place, not a chat transcript.
  • For leadershipSee what was chosen, what was deferred and why, and revisit it when new evidence arrives.

Already use ChatGPT or Claude? Keep using them to think and draft. PM OS gives the evidence, the decision and the deferred option a shared home your team can return to.
