Why it matters

Hard to explain what was never written down.

In an established product organisation, writers often learn what changed second-hand, from a ticket, a chat or a quick call. Guessing what was decided, or what was left out, is where mistakes get in.

  • For youLess guesswork about what was decided. Draft with the source in view.
  • For your teamFewer disconnected handoffs. Reviewers see the brief, the source and the draft together.
  • For leadershipA human review step stays visible before guidance reaches customers.

A general AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude can help you draft a paragraph. Writer OS is for the brief, the source and the review around it. Flags are advice. People verify claims and citations.
