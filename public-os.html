Why it matters

Hard to be clear without sounding like a promise.

Customers ask in many places and want a straight answer. Teams worry that a vote, or a roadmap card, will be read as a commitment.

  • For customersSee relevant information in plain words, and share feedback.
  • For your teamDecide what is appropriate to share, and say it clearly after a person has reviewed it.
  • For leadershipChoose what customers see. A roadmap shows intention, not a delivery date.

A chat assistant can help you phrase an update. Public OS is where the request, its status, the update and the feedback it brings stay together, and where a person decides who hears.

How it flows

From request to reply.

  1. RequestsIdeas, votes, comments
  2. ModerateReview, merge, keep it civil
  3. RoadmapCurated by your leaders
  4. UpdatesAuthored and published by you
  5. SendReview recipients, then send
  6. RepliesLogged for the next round of discovery
